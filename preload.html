Preload options

preload hints to the browser how much audio to load before playback. It does not start the audio.

  • auto — the browser may download the audio ahead of time.
  • metadata — load basic information, such as the duration, without downloading the entire audio.
  • none — do not preload audio data; wait until playback is requested.

The browser may choose how much to preload, so these values are suggestions.
